# Safe Harbor coverage

Which of the 18 HIPAA Safe Harbor identifier categories this tool looks for, and how much is known about how well. This is an assistant for human review, not a compliance certification: a person must review the output, and Safe Harbor also requires that nobody has actual knowledge that what remains could identify the person.

The categories follow 45 CFR 164.514(b)(2)(i) as summarized in secondary sources; check the regulation before relying on this table. Generated by `python -m deid.audit.coverage`; do not edit by hand.

Summary: 3 measured, 2 rule, 5 asked, 3 partial, 5 not covered.

| # | Category | Status | What the tool does |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Names | measured | NAME (patients, relatives, clinicians); scored on 2,587 labeled names in the 400 held-out notes. |
| 2 | Geographic subdivisions smaller than a state | partial | ADDRESS (street addresses, towns) is measured; ZIP codes after a state or the word ZIP are cut to three digits by a rule. County, precinct and facility names are not specifically tested. |
| 3 | Dates (except year) tied to a person, and ages over 89 | partial | DATE is measured. By default dates are shifted, which keeps intervals but is not the Safe Harbor form; `date_mode="year"` keeps only the year. Ages of 90 or more become "90+" by a rule (tested on invented text). |
| 4 | Telephone numbers | measured | PHONE. |
| 5 | Fax numbers | asked | PHONE (the prompt names fax numbers); 150 fax numbers are labeled in the 600-note set and scored together with the other phone numbers. |
| 6 | Email addresses | measured | EMAIL. |
| 7 | Social security numbers | asked | ID; 28 social security numbers are labeled in the 600-note set and scored together with the other IDs. |
| 8 | Medical record numbers | asked | ID; scored together with the other IDs (600 MRNs labeled in the 600-note set). |
| 9 | Health plan beneficiary numbers | asked | ID (insurance numbers); scored together with the other IDs. |
| 10 | Account numbers | asked | ID; scored together with the other IDs. |
| 11 | Certificate and license numbers | not covered | Not in the prompt and not tested. |
| 12 | Vehicle identifiers and license plates | not covered | Not in the prompt and not tested. |
| 13 | Device identifiers and serial numbers | not covered | Not in the prompt and not tested. |
| 14 | Web addresses (URLs) | rule | Found by a rule (http, https and www addresses) and replaced with example.org. |
| 15 | IP addresses | rule | Found by a rule (four dotted numbers, each 0-255) and replaced from the documentation block 192.0.2.x. |
| 16 | Biometric identifiers, including finger and voice prints | not covered | Dictated audio is transcribed to text; voice prints are not handled. |
| 17 | Full-face photographs and comparable images | not covered | Scanned pages are read as text only; photographs are not looked for. |
| 18 | Any other unique identifying number, characteristic or code | partial | The re-identification reviewer lists clues (rare conditions, occupation, small places) for a person; they are not edited automatically. Local codes can be added with a site profile. |

Status words: **measured** = scored on the project's synthetic sets; **rule** = found by a rule with no model, tested on invented text only; **asked** = the model is asked for it but no result is reported for this category alone; **partial** = only part of the category; **not covered** = not looked for.
