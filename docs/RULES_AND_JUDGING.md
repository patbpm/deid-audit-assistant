# Rules, judging and prizes (summary of the official Participant Guide)

Always check the Participant Guide and Discord #announcements for changes. This is a summary, not the official text.

## Rules
- Repositories must be public.
- Team size up to 4; solo allowed. Teams submit one project; appoint one Representative.
- New work only: what you present must be built during the event. The demo may highlight only features, code and functionality built during the hackathon. Failing to identify your original contributions means immediate disqualification at the judges' discretion. (Startups may build on existing products if the new work is clearly identified.)
- Banned: projects that violate legal, ethical or platform policies, or that use code, data or assets you do not have the rights to.
- Sample anti-projects to avoid: AI that generates and gives medical advice; basic RAG apps; simple image analyzers; simple "AI for Education" chatbots; AI job-application screeners; AI nutrition coaches; personality analyzers; sports analyzers or coaches; anything political or involving war.
- Project must build toward a problem statement and must incorporate Meta's models.
- Eligibility (from the application page, still to confirm): "government-affiliated individuals" are excluded and the term is not defined. Ask in Discord #questions whether a state-university research assistant counts. Keep the reply.

## Problem statements
- Build for Your World: bring Meta's models into your domain; solve a problem you know firsthand.
- Push the Frontier: give a machine a new capability in software, robotics, manufacturing, energy, materials and beyond.

## Submission
- Submit on the Cerebral Valley platform. Late submissions are not considered.
- Required: public repository, three-minute demo video (accessible), everyone who worked on it listed.
- The video is a primary artifact judges use, along with the code and live product.

## Judging
- Stage 1 (pass/fail): reasonably fits a problem statement and reasonably uses Meta Model API and/or Meta open-weight models.
- Stage 2 (0-10 each): Impact 30%; Use of Meta AI Tooling 25% (creative, beyond a basic integration); Technical Depth and Execution 20%; Demo 25%.
- Stage 3: top 5 per track, watched live by final-round judges using the same rubric.

- Bonus ($50K reserved): Most Viral Project $20K (X engagement until Nov 8); Best Build on Meta Open Weight Models $10K (Muse Glimmer or Muse Spark Open on own hardware or partner inference); Community Vote $10K; Best Agentic Use-Case $5K; Best Creative Build $5K (Muse Image or Muse Wonder).
